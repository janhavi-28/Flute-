GOOGLE OAUTH SETUP GUIDE FOR FLUTEROOTS

STEP 1: CREATE OR SELECT A PROJECT
1. Go to the Google Cloud Console: https://console.cloud.google.com/
2. Look at the top left of your screen, next to the "Google Cloud" logo. Click the button that currently says "GreenRemote Auth" (or whatever project is selected).
3. In the window that pops up, click "NEW PROJECT" in the top right corner.
4. Name the project "FluteRoots Auth" and click Create.
5. Wait a few seconds, then make sure "FluteRoots Auth" is selected in that top-left dropdown.

STEP 2: SET UP THE "OAUTH CONSENT SCREEN"
1. In the middle of your screen under "Quick access", click the "APIs and services" button. 
   (If you don't see it, click the three horizontal lines at the very top-left to open the menu, then click "APIs & Services").
2. On the left-hand menu, click "OAuth consent screen".
3. Choose "External" (this allows anyone on the internet to log in) and click "Create".
4. Fill out the required fields:
   - App name: FluteRoots
   - User support email: (Select your email from the dropdown)
   - Developer contact information: (Type your email again at the very bottom)
5. Click "Save and Continue" at the bottom of the next 3 screens (Scopes, Test Users, Summary) until you reach the end.
6. CRITICAL STEP: Back on the main OAuth consent screen page, click the "PUBLISH APP" button. If you skip this, only you will be able to log in!

STEP 3: GET THE CREDENTIALS
1. On the left-hand menu, click "Credentials".
2. At the top of the screen, click the "+ CREATE CREDENTIALS" button.
3. Select "OAuth client ID" from the dropdown.
4. For Application type, select "Web application".
5. Name it "Supabase Web Client".
6. Scroll down to "Authorized redirect URIs" and click the "ADD URI" button.
7. Paste EXACTLY this link into the box: 
   https://wfyvndcialfvggzpooyv.supabase.co/auth/v1/callback
8. Click "Create".

STEP 4: PUT THE KEYS INTO SUPABASE
1. A box will pop up displaying your "Client ID" and "Client Secret". Keep this tab open.
2. Open a new tab and go to your Supabase Dashboard -> Authentication -> Providers.
3. Click on "Google" to open its settings.
4. Toggle "Enable Google" to the ON position.
5. Copy the Client ID from Google and paste it into Supabase.
6. Copy the Client Secret from Google and paste it into Supabase.
7. Click Save in Supabase.

You are done! The Google login button on your website will now work perfectly.
